# BOOKING STATE MACHINE
######################################################################
The script looked for a STAGES/STAGE_NAMES/STAGE_LABELS/STAGE_MAP
constant and didn't find one -- that's a naming mismatch, not missing
data. There are actually TWO separate, real mappings, covering two
different concepts that the script's search conflated into one:

1) current_stage_index -> stage label (a UI progress-bar position,
   e.g. "step 3 of 5" -- purely a display label, does not drive any
   business logic). Defined as DONOR_STAGES / RECIPIENT_STAGES class
   attributes on MilkBankRequest (milkbank/models.py), selected via
   request_type through the `stages` property:

   DONOR_STAGES = [
       0: "Status",
       1: "Booking Confirmation",
       2: "Counseling and Testing",  # renamed 2026-09-07, was "Counseling & Serology Screening" --
                                      # the Fabella interview established this stage covers serology,
                                      # physical, AND blood tests, not serology alone
       3: "Breastmilk Analysis",
       4: "Results",
   ]
   RECIPIENT_STAGES = [
       0: "Requirements",
       1: "Status",
       2: "Booking Confirmation",
       3: "Results",
   ]

   current_stage_index is incremented in milkbank/views.py at specific
   real actions (not automatically on every status change):
     - ConfirmAttendanceView: +1 when the mother confirms attendance
     - AcceptCounterOfferView: +1 when she accepts a facility's
       counter-offered date
     - StaffConfirmCompletionView: jumps straight to the last stage
       (len(stages) - 1) when staff mark the booking completed

2) current_sub_status -> the REAL state machine that actually gates
   what can happen next. Defined as MilkBankRequest.Status (choices)
   plus ALLOWED_TRANSITIONS in milkbank/transitions.py -- the single
   place status is ever allowed to change (apply_transition()), which
   also fires the matching notification and writes an audit log entry.

   Allowed transitions (current -> allowed next):
     pending            -> awaiting_attendance, declined, expired
     awaiting_attendance -> scheduled, counter_offered, expired
     counter_offered    -> scheduled, pending
     scheduled          -> completed
     declined           -> (terminal, no further moves)
     expired            -> (terminal, no further moves)
     completed          -> (terminal, no further moves)

   Notification sent to the mother on each transition
   (STATUS_NOTIFICATIONS in transitions.py):
     awaiting_attendance -> "The facility accepted your request. Please confirm your attendance."
     scheduled           -> "Your appointment is scheduled."
     declined            -> "The facility declined your request."
     expired             -> "Your request has expired."
     counter_offered     -> "The facility proposed a new date for your appointment."
     completed           -> "Your booking is complete. Thank you!"

   Side effects wired into apply_transition() itself:
     - declined/expired/completed (TERMINAL_STATUSES) atomically
       decrement Facility.booked_count via F() (race-safe under
       concurrent completions)
     - completed also creates a TransactionRecord (Donation or
       Received, based on request_type)

   current_stage_index and current_sub_status are independent axes --
   e.g. a request can sit at stage_index=1 ("Booking Confirmation")
   while current_sub_status cycles through awaiting_attendance ->
   counter_offered -> scheduled without the stage index moving at all.

######################################################################
# BOOKING STATE MACHINE
######################################################################
DONOR stages (current_stage_index -> name):
  0 = Status
  1 = Booking Confirmation
  2 = Counseling and Testing
  3 = Breastmilk Analysis
  4 = Results

RECIPIENT stages (current_stage_index -> name):
  0 = Requirements
  1 = Status
  2 = Booking Confirmation
  3 = Results

Allowed status transitions (transitions.ALLOWED_TRANSITIONS):
  pending              -> awaiting_attendance, counter_offered, declined, expired, scheduled
  awaiting_attendance  -> counter_offered, expired, scheduled
  counter_offered      -> awaiting_attendance, pending, scheduled
  scheduled            -> awaiting_attendance, completed, declined
  declined             -> (final, no further moves)
  expired              -> (final, no further moves)
  completed            -> (final, no further moves)

Final statuses (set closed_at): completed, declined, expired
Statuses with an 8-business-hour response clock: awaiting_attendance, pending

Notification sent to the mother on reaching each status (transitions.STATUS_NOTIFICATIONS):
  awaiting_attendance  "The facility accepted your request. Please confirm your attendance."
  scheduled            "Your appointment is scheduled."
  declined             "The facility declined your request."
  expired              "Your request has expired."
  counter_offered      "The facility proposed a new date for your appointment."
  completed            "Your booking is complete. Thank you!"

Every transition goes through transitions.apply_transition(), which also writes
the audit log; reaching 'completed' creates a TransactionRecord and moves the
facility's stock_level_ml and the mother's lifetime total by the recorded mL.
current_stage_index and current_sub_status are independent: a request can sit
at one stage while its status changes.
